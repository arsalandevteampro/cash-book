Cash Book: Smart Daily Khata & Financial Ledger

An offline-first, lightning-fast personal and small business bookkeeping application built with Flutter. Empowers shopkeepers, freelancers, and individuals to manage cash flows, track customer debts (udhaar) with instant WhatsApp reminders, set monthly savings targets, and securely sync encrypted backups to Google Drive.

Current Version 1.1.0 (Build 21)
Architecture Offline-First Hive + Riverpod
Primary Target Android (Google Play Ready)
Design Language Material 3 / Teal Green
🎯

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

⚠️ The Core Problem
Millions of retail store owners, street vendors, and individuals still rely on fragile paper notebooks (Khata/Bahi-Khata) or clunky spreadsheets. They suffer from uncollected debt, forgotten payables, lack of real-time balance calculations, accidental data loss, and privacy breaches when leaving physical registers unattended.
💡 The Cash Book Solution
A 100% offline-ready, high-performance ledger application with zero subscription barriers. It integrates instant debt follow-ups via WhatsApp/SMS, biometric pattern privacy, multi-book isolation (Home vs. Shop), and AES-256 encrypted Google Drive cloud backups.
👥

🏪 Local Retailer (Tariq)

🔄 Feature Optimization: High-Performance Filter Bottom Sheet (V2)

P0 Performance & UX

Problem Diagnosis: Profiling revealed that tapping any filter chip inside _showFilterBottomSheet directly invoked _HomeScreenState.setState(), forcing the entire ledger list (500+ transactions), running balance math, and chart aggregates to recompute in the background on every tap, causing frame drops. In addition, large category collections pushed the 'Apply Filters' button off-screen, changes mutated state without confirmation, and users lacked match visibility.

❌ BEFORE (v1 Bottlenecks & Friction)
  • Background Recompute Lag: Every chip tap called parent setState(), recalculating hundreds of transactions and re-rendering the home screen while the modal was open.
  • Uncommitted State Mutation: Filters updated immediately on tap. Dismissing or swiping away the sheet retained unintended filter selections.
  • Lost Off-Screen Apply Button: 'Apply Filters' sat at the bottom of an unconstrained scroll view, pushed off-screen by 20+ category chips.
  • Blind Filtering: Users had zero feedback on whether their filter combination matched any transactions until after closing the sheet.
  • Plain Text Chips: Payment methods and transaction types lacked icons and modern segmented styling.
✨ AFTER (v2 Optimized Architecture)
  • Zero-Lag Local Draft State: All selections mutate local draft variables (tempPeriod, tempCategories, tempPayments, tempType). Parent screen only updates upon clicking 'Apply'.
  • Safe Dismissal: Swiping down or tapping cancel cleanly discards draft changes without affecting active filters.
  • Sticky Footer Action Bar: Pinned bottom bar with [Clear All] on the left and primary [Apply Filters] on the right, always accessible.
  • Live Match Counter: Apply button dynamically calculates and displays live matching results in real-time (e.g. Apply Filters (18 matches)).
  • Modern M3 Styling: 3-way segmented type toggle (All, 🟢 Income, 🔴 Expense) and iconized payment chips (Cash 💵, Card 💳, Bank 🏦, UPI 📱).

⚠️ CRITICAL DEFECT REPORT: Follow-Up Interstitial Ad Show-Rate Drop (0.43%)
High Revenue Impact

Production metrics for ad unit C_F_ups_Interstitial (ca-app-pub-7531348818625320/2870273086) reveal an acute discrepancy between matched ad requests (466) and successfully rendered impressions (only 2), yielding a catastrophic 0.43% Show Rate. Despite an extraordinary eCPM of $41.60, total revenue is restricted to $0.08 due to 464 unrendered and expired ads.

Ad Unit Requests Matched (Fill) Impressions Show Rate Observed eCPM Diagnosis
C_F_ups_Interstitial 466 466 (100%) 2 0.43% $41.60 Severe Lag / Premature Pop
C_E_R_Interstitial (Reports) 488 488 (100%) 18 3.69% $3.81 Preloaded on Screen Entry
CashBook_Banner_Android 2,056 1,014 (49.3%) 800 78.90% $0.21 Healthy Inline Baseline

🔍 Root Cause Analysis: Where is the Lag & Drop-off Happening?

1. Premature Global Preload in main.dart
main.dart:53 calls FollowUpInterstitialAdManager.preloadAd() unconditionally on every single app launch. Users opening the app for balance checks never create a follow-up; AdMob 1-hour expiration TTL kills the loaded ad in background, artificially inflating requests to 466 with 0 impressions.
2. Missing Preload on Form Screen Entry
AddFollowUpScreen.initState() has zero preload logic. Users spend 15 to 45 seconds filling out contact name, amount, and date/time. This valuable form-filling duration is completely wasted instead of silently caching the ad in background.
3. Double HTTP 204 Network Ping Latency
When "Save" is tapped, add_follow_up_screen.dart:149 executes ConnectivityHelper.hasInternetConnection() (2-3s). Immediately after, showAdThen():80 executes the exact same HTTP check a second time. Back-to-back TCP/TLS handshakes create 4-6s cumulative lag, triggering false offline fallbacks.
4. 5-Second Timeout Race Condition
Because no ad is ready, showAdThen() attempts an on-demand load with a 5-second timeout. Double internet ping (3s) + AdMob network delivery (3-5s) exceeds the 5s ceiling. The timeout triggers onComplete(), which executes Navigator.pop() and closes the screen. The ad finishes loading 2s later into an empty graveyard with no visible context.
5. Redundant Internet Pre-Flight on Already Cached Ads
If an ad is already loaded (_interstitialAd != null), its creative and video assets are already cached locally on the device. Testing internet via Google 204 before calling ad.show() is architecturally redundant and actively blocks pre-cached ads from rendering whenever mobile connectivity fluctuates.

💡 Architecture Solution & Remediation Strategy (Target: 75%+ Show Rate)

  • Contextual Lifecycle Preloading: Remove preload from main.dart. Call FollowUpInterstitialAdManager.instance.preloadAd() inside AddFollowUpScreen.initState(). The ad will load silently while the user types, guaranteeing 0ms wait on Save.
  • Zero-Latency Instant Render: In showAdThen(), check if (_interstitialAd != null) immediately at line 1. Render the cached ad directly without any preceding network verification.
  • Single-Point Connectivity Verification: Rely exclusively on the internet check already performed during form validation in _saveFollowUp(). Do not run duplicate HTTP socket pings in the ad manager.
  • Safety Fallback & Smooth User Feedback: If a user saves so fast that the ad is still loading, show a brief progress overlay ("Finalizing...") instead of popping the screen and abandoning the ad mid-flight.
  • Automatic Post-Dismissal Refresh: Preload the next ad in onAdDismissedFullScreenContent so subsequent edits within the same session remain instantaneous.

🔄 Feature Upgrade: More Apps & Closed Testing Access Hub (V2 Architecture)

P0 Priority

Problem Diagnosis: Users submitted testing requests that were approved in Google Play Console, but active beta testers failed to increase. Investigation revealed two root causes: (1) In Settings, a top-level "Beta Testing Access" banner misled users into believing all apps required an access request; (2) Tapping the approval push notification only executed debugPrint and opened the default home ledger without any download link or Google account guidance.

❌ BEFORE (v1 Architecture & Friction Points)
  • Misleading Top Banner: Prominent "Beta Testing Access" banner sat above all apps in Settings, causing users to believe even public Play Store apps were locked and required access requests.
  • Mixed App Portfolio: Public and closed testing apps were mixed in one flat list with only a tiny "Beta" pill, confusing casual users.
  • No Notification Click Action: Tapping the approval push notification executed only debugPrint and opened the Cash Book daily ledger. Zero deep linking or download triggers.
  • No Google Account Guidance: Users opened Play Store under a different personal Google account and received "Item not found" errors, abandoning installation.
  • Ambiguous Access Scope: Testers were uncertain whether access was per-app or portfolio-wide.
✨ AFTER (v2 High-Conversion Testing Architecture)
  • Clean Visual Separation: Public Play Store apps separated under "Our Apps on Google Play" with direct green [Install on Play Store] button. Zero requests, zero barrier.
  • Universal 1-Approval Rule: Adding the user's email once to Play Console automatically grants universal access to all closed testing apps.
  • Interactive Notification Tap Handler: Tapping the push notification instantly displays a dedicated "Beta Testing Hub" Pop-up / Bottom Sheet directly on top of the app.
  • Prominent Account Guidance Banner: Highlighted alert inside the popup clearly displays the approved email (user@gmail.com) instructing the user to ensure Google Play Store is switched to this account.
  • Direct 1-Click Install List: Modal lists all active testing apps with direct [📥 Install on Play Store] action buttons opening testing opt-in URLs.
